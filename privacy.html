Privacy policy

strix-gdrive-sync is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: files in the owner's Google Drive, read-only, through the Google Drive API.
  • What it does with them: copies them to the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other person, company or server.
  • What it collects: nothing. There is no analytics, tracking, advertising or sharing.
  • Google user data is used only to provide this copy, and in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
